Meet Rhetorix

A platform for ideas, not just arguments.

Debate formats can be adapted to the event, experience level, and time available. These are formats Rhetorix can host in future editions.

⚖️

One-on-One Debate

Two speakers take opposing sides of a motion. Each must present claims, support them with reasoning, and respond to the other side.

Direct • Focused • Competitive
👥

Team Debate

Teams divide roles, build a shared case, rebut opposing arguments, and work together to deliver a coherent position.

Collaboration • Strategy
🔄

Rebuttal Round

Participants listen closely and respond to specific points made by opponents, rather than simply repeating prepared speeches.

Listening • Quick thinking
🎲

Impromptu Debate

Speakers receive a topic shortly before speaking. The challenge is to organise thoughts quickly and make a persuasive case.

Spontaneity • Clarity
🗣️

Parliamentary Style

Speakers argue for or against a motion using defined roles, timed speeches, points of rebuttal, and structured rounds.

Structure • Persuasion
🌍

Open Discussion

A moderated exchange of views on a relevant issue, designed to explore nuance and understand competing perspectives.

Perspective • Dialogue
From registration to results

How a Rhetorix debate works.

Every event may have its own rules, but a clear structure helps every participant know what to expect.

Register & get briefed

Participants receive the event details, eligibility information, format, schedule, and code of conduct.

Pairings & motion

Speakers are paired or placed into teams. The topic and speaking positions are communicated according to the event rules.

Present & rebut

Each side delivers its arguments within time limits, responds to opposing points, and follows moderator instructions.

Evaluation & results

Judges assess reasoning, evidence, rebuttal, clarity, and delivery. Results and feedback are shared after the round.

What makes a strong debater?

Judged on substance and skill.

A transparent rubric helps participants understand what matters beyond simply speaking loudly or quickly.

🧩

Logic & argument

Is the position clear, relevant, consistent, and supported by sound reasoning?

📚

Evidence & examples

Are examples relevant and claims explained responsibly rather than asserted without support?

↩️

Rebuttal & listening

Does the speaker directly engage with opposing arguments and answer the central points?

🎤

Delivery & clarity

Is the speech understandable, well-paced, organised, and appropriate for the audience?

⏱️

Time & structure

Does the speaker follow time limits, stay on topic, and organise the speech effectively?

🌿

Conduct & respect

Does the participant challenge ideas respectfully and follow the moderator's instructions?
